4Capture the Epic screen

Epic Eyes will infer where you are from the screenshot. The screenshot and your question will be placed together in one PDF.

Default capture method: Windows Snipping Tool.
You can also drag and drop an image into this box.
One-file rule: the screenshot is embedded into the same Epic_Eyes_Payload.pdf as your role and question. You do not need to paste the image separately into Epic Eyes.

5Open Epic Eyes + make the PDF

This is one action. The page creates one PDF containing your structured prompt + screenshot, downloads it, and opens the dedicated Epic Eyes agent.
Add your screenshot and question, then open Epic Eyes.
In Epic Eyes: attach Epic_Eyes_Payload.pdf, then type a short question after the PDF and send it. Example: “What should I click next?” If Microsoft still does not enable Send, a single ? can be used as the shortest trigger.
Your role, full question, Epic Eyes instructions, and screenshot will be combined into one PDF.
